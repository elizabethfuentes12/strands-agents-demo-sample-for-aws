# Canva image prompts

Paleta de marca (del sitio oficial de Strands): fondo oscuro `#0e0e0e` / `#17181c`,
verde acento `#00cc5f`, verde brillante `#00ff77`, índigo `#3d50f5`, texto `#f2f3f5`.
Estilo general: dark mode, glow verde neón sutil, tipografía sans-serif bold, look
"developer tool" moderno (como una terminal elegante), sin fotos de stock.

> act, and get blocked — live". Right side: a stylized split-screen UI mockup —
> a chat bubble panel on the left and a glowing panel of small cards on the
> right (cards labeled with a wrench icon, a red shield "BLOCKED", a green
> checkmark), connected by thin animated-looking green lines. Bottom-right
> corner: small AWS smile logo and a hexagon mesh. Flat vector style, neon
> green glow accents, no photos, developer-tool aesthetic.
